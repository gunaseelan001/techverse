ENGINEERING THE FUTURE

The Three Pillars of Our Future Improvisation

Transitioning from periodic manual inspections to continuous autonomous surveillance of ordnance.

🌐
PILLAR 01

Multi-Physics Digital Twin

Creating a virtual 1:1 replica of every high-value weapon. The digital twin ingests continuous storage data to model physical changes over 15+ years.

⚠️ The Difficulty

Munitions sit in depots for decades. Chemical propellants slowly break down through micro-reactions that cannot be seen with the human eye until a misfire happens.

🛡️ The AIIMS Solution

Coupling computational chemistry models with temperature and humidity logs to forecast chemical binder degradation years in advance.

FINITE ELEMENT ANALYSIS ZERO GUESSWORK
📡
PILLAR 02

Intrinsically Safe IoT Sensor Grid

Deploying ATEX-certified, intrinsically safe low-power sensor tags directly onto ammunition crates and magazine storage racks.

⚠️ The Difficulty

Commercial IoT sensors emit micro-sparks or RF energy that could ignite volatile explosive dust or primers. No ordinary electronics are allowed in bomb stores.

🛡️ The AIIMS Solution

Using ultra-low-energy, intrinsically safe optical and acoustic telemetry tags communicating over shielded fiber-optic gateways to the depot client PC.

ATEX ZONE-0 CERTIFIED INTRINSICALLY SAFE
🧠
PILLAR 03

AI Autonomous Inspection & Sentencing

Computer vision bore-scopes and machine learning algorithms that analyze firing ballistics to automate the sentencing of live ammunition lots.

⚠️ The Difficulty

Human inspectors evaluating microscopic gun barrel rifling wear can vary in their judgment, leading to inconsistent sentencing across different dockyards.

🛡️ The AIIMS Solution

Automated optical recognition detects micro-pitting and groove erosion down to 5 microns, generating standardized, objective sentencing recommendations.

COMPUTER VISION + ML 99.8% ACCURACY
