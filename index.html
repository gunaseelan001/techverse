⚠️ Why Handling Lakhs of Armament Data in Navy is Exceptionally Tough

In commercial software, if an inventory count has a temporary lag or glitch, it's a minor inconvenience. In naval armament operations:

  • Zero Error Tolerance: A microscopic tolerance deviation in tube clearance or an unrecorded propellant moisture variation can cause weapon jamming or firing failure at sea.
  • Air-Gapped & Distributed: The system operates across distributed coastal naval depots with no public cloud or internet access. Everything runs on secured local naval servers.
  • Decades of Ballistic History: Every ammunition lot carries years of proof firing trials, SRF reliability coefficients, and chemical stability certifications.

5. Calibrations & Metrology Flows
