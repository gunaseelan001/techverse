The Complete Armament Management Ecosystem

MANUFACTURING

1. Production of Arms & Armaments

End-to-end tracking of pyrotechnic manufacturing, explosive loading, casing machining, and multi-tier Product Tree (BOM) hierarchy down to sub-gram propellant grains.

Pyrotechnic Complexity: Munitions contain hundreds of sub-components and hazardous chemical batches. A single defective primer ruins an entire lot of 10,000 shells.
Genealogy Tracking: AIIMS tracks lot genealogy down to raw chemical batches, automatically isolating affected munitions with 1-click recall workflows.

PROCUREMENT

2. Orders & Defence Contracts

Managing complex defence tenders, vendor delivery milestones, Factory Acceptance Tests (FAT), and seamless transition from contract indents to physical depot stock.

Multi-Agency Red Tape: Vendor milestone tracking across Defence Public Sector Undertakings (DPSUs) often suffers from lost paperwork and untracked deviation concessions.
Automated Milestone Verification: Digital sign-offs tie delivery clearance directly to quality acceptance checksheets, preventing payment release without quality approval.

FIELD HARDWARE

3. Secure Rugged Devices

Waterproof, shock-resistant tablets deployed directly in magazine vaults and submarine berths. Manages zero-downtime binary updates and companion sync daemons.

Submarine Isolation: How do you update software on rugged tablets inside an isolated submarine base without internet or cloud app stores?
Port 9090 Sync Daemon: Automated local network distribution from NCN VM to Client PCs, verifying SHA-256 hashes with instant rollback if health check fails.
PORT 9090 SYNC & ROLLBACK Deep Dive →
📋
INSPECTION WORKFLOW

4. Digital Checksheets & Workflows

Dynamic multi-point inspection forms for torpedo tubes, gun mounts, and missile launchers with instant tolerance checks and digital sign-offs.

Human Error in Paper Forms: A single unreadable handwritten number on a torpedo tube bore measurement could lead to catastrophic firing failure at sea.
Real-Time Range Validation: AIIMS enforces strict mathematical bounds (e.g. 533.4mm ± 0.05mm), instantly highlighting anomalies before records can be submitted.
TORPEDO & GUN CHECKSHEETS Deep Dive →
📐
PRECISION METROLOGY

5. Calibrations & Metrology

Tracking thousands of master gauges, pressure transducers, and acoustic hydrophones. Enforces NABL calibration cycles and deviation concession approvals.

Measurement Drift: If a testing instrument goes out of calibration, every weapon tested with it is legally uncertified and must be recalled.
Automated Periodicity Locking: Instruments automatically lock when calibration expires, preventing inspectors from issuing test certificates with uncalibrated gear.
NABL TRACEABILITY MATRIX Deep Dive →

EXECUTIVE COMMAND

6. Dashboards Monitoring Everything

Holistic Command HUD providing Flag Officers with instant situational awareness of fleet war-reserves, active inspection queues, and depot inventory heatmaps.

Information Overload: Admirals cannot read thousands of raw inspection sheets; they need instant high-level readiness with one-click drilldowns.
Live Aggregation Pipeline: High-speed telemetry synthesizes lakhs of raw rows into clear combat readiness indicators across Eastern, Western, and Southern Commands.
