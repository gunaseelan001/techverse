METROLOGY & QUALITY ASSURANCE

Digital Checksheets & NABL Calibrations

Replacing slow, error-prone paper binders with smart digital checksheets that actively prevent out-of-tolerance entries and enforce NABL instrument calibration.

📋 Real-Time Mathematical Bounds
Enforces strict tolerances on torpedo tube bore diameters (±0.05mm) and gun barrel rifling wear, instantly flagging anomalies.
📐 Automated Periodicity Locking
Expired testing gauges are locked in the database—preventing inspectors from certifying munitions using uncalibrated instruments.
✍️ Multi-Tier Digital Sign-Offs
Dual-control approvals prevent an inspector from certifying their own checksheet, ensuring legal and military integrity.
